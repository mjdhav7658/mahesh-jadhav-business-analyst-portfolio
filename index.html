Titan Award · Analytics owner

Top 1,000 sellers: finding the real driver of contacts

Problem
Seller support contacts were rising and costly. Data sat in three systems with different category names. Goal: cut contacts per 1,000 units sold by 10%.
What I did
Reconciled the category taxonomies and joined 44.6M contacts with sales and seller data in SQL. Segmented sellers by region, then scored them on outreach, sales and inventory to pick who to contact in advance. Measured the contacted group before and after outreach.
Result
Contacts per 1,000 units fell from 32 to 24, a 25% cut against a 10% goal.

Empty Chair Award · Owned end to end

Seller AI Assistant: measuring an AI product

Problem
Leadership was piloting an AI assistant for sellers in one category and needed to know if it was efficient enough to scale to other categories.
What I did
Brainstormed metrics with the product team and agreed four, with written definitions. Worked with data engineers to pull the tool's data through APIs and combined it with operational data. Grouped seller conversations by the team responsible (fraud, abuse, feedback, monitoring) using keyword rules and Bedrock summaries, then built dashboards comparing the assistant with human agents.
Result
Gave leadership the evidence to decide on scaling beyond the pilot.

Power BI

Quality dashboard with root causes

Problem
Audit results were exported by hand from an internal tool, so quality metrics were late and leaders couldn't see where defects came from.
What I did
Automated the exports through SQL ETL into Redshift and built a Power BI dashboard showing defects per million opportunities (DPMO) weekly and monthly by country, department and manager, with a three-level root cause breakdown.
Result
Metrics that weren't visible before; 0.3 headcount of reporting effort saved and one day less reporting delay. Used in performance and business reviews.
