Case studies
1. Seller contact reduction: Titan Award
Problem: seller contacts were rising; the goal was a 10% cut in contacts per 1,000 units.
What I did: joined 44.6M contacts with sales and seller data in SQL and found that 1,000 of 1.5M sellers drove 35% of contacts. Built the targeting logic and measured results before and after outreach.
Result: contacts per 1,000 units fell from 32 to 24 (−25%).
Tech: SQL, AWS Redshift, QuickSight
2. Seller AI Assistant measurement: Empty Chair Award
Problem: was the AI assistant efficient enough to scale to other product categories?
What I did: defined four success metrics with the product team (adoption, contact reduction, and speed and quality versus human agents) and built the analysis comparing the assistant with human agents.
Result: leadership used the evidence to decide on scaling.
Tech: SQL, Redshift, QuickSight, Amazon Bedrock
3. Customer experience: first contact resolution
Problem: too many issues weren't solved on the first contact.
What I did: cohort analysis by seller sales and product category to find where repeat contacts came from, tracked on a dashboard.
Result: first contact resolution 80% → 87%; repeat contacts 20% → 15%.
Tech: SQL, cohort analysis, QuickSight
4. Reporting platform migration: Process Ninja Award
Problem: move the reporting platform to a new Redshift cluster in one month without disruption.
What I did: mapped dependencies across 23 pipelines and 20 dashboards, rebuilt tables and tested outputs side by side before cutover.
Result: zero disruption for 300+ daily users; a critical pipeline went from 8 hours to 1.5.
Tech: AWS Redshift, S3, Glue, PySpark
5. Quality dashboard with root causes
Problem: audit results were exported by hand, so quality metrics were late.
What I did: automated exports through SQL ETL into Redshift and built a Power BI dashboard showing defects per million opportunities by country, department and manager, with a three-level root cause drill-down.
Result: saved 0.3 headcount of reporting effort and one day of reporting delay.
Tech: Power BI, DAX, SQL, Redshift

Note: the quality dashboard on the site uses illustrative values; the real data is confidential.
